Document Slate is a free office suite for Android that respects your privacy. No ads, no tracking, no accounts and no built-in AI. The app has no internet permission at all: your files never leave your phone.

One app, four tools:

* Word Slate: documents (DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT; opens DOC)
* Grid Slate: spreadsheets (XLSX, XLS, XLSB, ODS, CSV)
* Deck Slate: presentations (PPTX, PPSX) with slideshow
* Page Slate: PDF viewing, comments, forms, fill and sign, page tools

Features:

* Files open in a fast viewer; editing loads only when you tap Edit
* Touch-friendly edit tabs for text, paragraphs, cells, formulas, slides and shapes
* Spelling check with offline dictionaries
* Print to printers on your local network through Android's print system
* Save, Save as and export to PDF
* Full-fidelity guard: the app never silently drops pictures, charts or other content when it saves
* Certificate signatures for PDFs, with keys kept in the Android Keystore
* Create QR codes and barcodes, and tap codes and links in documents to read or open them
* Dark and light themes

Document Slate is built on the open-source ArtCraft document engines (MIT OR Apache-2.0). It is not affiliated with ArtCraft.
